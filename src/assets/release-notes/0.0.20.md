# Highlights

- **New:** You can now open multiple windows to easily manage multiple chat sessions.
- The account section has been redesigned for a more intuitive experience.
- We resolved some issues with connecting your account to the desktop app.
- In your account settings on the web, you can now update your username, change your email or password, and view your connected sign-in methods.

# Miscellaneous Changes

- Profile avatars are now generated locally using Boring Avatars based on your account ID, so no avatar data is fetched or stored.
